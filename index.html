Integration Guide — Demo → Live
Connect this dashboard to the real car in 5 steps. Use the same HiveMQ credentials as your existing pit dashboard.
1
Find your HiveMQ credentials
Check your existing pit dashboard settings. You need: broker hostname, port (8884), username, password.
2
Enter credentials here
Click the ⚙ gear icon (top right). Fill in Broker Host, Port=8884, Username, Password. Click SAVE.
3
Switch to LIVE MQTT
Click the LIVE MQTT button in the top bar. The dot should turn green and show "Live".
4
Verify connection
Check the Data Dictionary panel — channels A (speed) and N (FC temp) should show real values. If the dot stays amber, check your broker hostname.
5
Strategy topic (optional)
If running the strategy engine, it publishes plans to thaiger/<car>/strategy. This dashboard subscribes automatically and renders the plan on the strip + charts.
